# Privacy Policy & Terms (drafting hygiene)

**Not legal advice.** Helps the agent structure pages and spot gaps. Material risk → counsel.
Triggers: “privacy policy”, “terms and conditions”, “cookie policy”, “ scrivi i termini”.

There is **no separate** `privacy-policy` / `terms` skill in this kit — stay here so
GDPR, cookies, and processors stay consistent with one evidence pack.

## Modes

| Mode | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| `gap-review` | Diff live pages vs reality (processors, features) |
| `outline` | Section outline for counsel or founder draft |
| `stub` | Honest MVP stub for template sites (vertical packs) — labeled as needs review |
| `version-bump` | Hand off date/version to `release-versioning` policy-versions |

## Privacy Policy — minimum sections (EU-leaning SaaS / local sites)

1. Who we are (controller identity, contact)  
2. What data (categories)  
3. Why / legal bases (per purpose — don’t invent LI without thought)  
4. Cookies / similar (or link Cookie notice)  
5. Recipients / processors (honest list)  
6. Transfers outside EEA (if any)  
7. Retention  
8. Rights (access, erasure, …) + how to request  
9. Children (if relevant)  
10. Changes + effective date  

Local vitrine (base pack): still need identity, contacts, what the form collects, processors (host, email), rights contact.

## Terms — minimum sections

1. Who / agreement to terms  
2. Service description (honest — “pre-release” if alpha)  
3. Accounts / acceptable use  
4. Fees / renewals / cancel (if paid)  
5. IP  
6. Liability limits (fair; counsel for consumer)  
7. Governing law / disputes  
8. Contact  
9. Effective date  

## Hard rules

1. Never claim “GDPR compliant” as a badge from a template.  
2. Generators are starting points — replace processors with **yours**.  
3. Store/App paywall links must match live URLs (`store-checklist`).  
4. Version/date hygiene → `release-versioning`.
